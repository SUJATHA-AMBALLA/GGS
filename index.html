One day to imagine a better neighbourhood.

What would you change in your neighbourhood if you had one day, a team, and the tools to think like an innovator?

From safer streets to greener mobility, from healthier public spaces to smarter local services — this is your chance to design a solution that could make your neighbourhood better.

Challenge description

The challenge: Fix your neighborhood!

Cities are full of small problems that affect daily life:

unsafe crossings · traffic around schools · lack of green spaces · difficult public transport connections · polluted streets · public spaces that do not feel welcoming

During the hackathon, each team chooses one real neighbourhood problem and creates a smart, sustainable and people-friendly solution.

Your idea should answer:

  1. What is the problem?
  2. Who is affected by it?
  3. How could STEM, digital tools or smart city thinking help?
  4. How would the solution make the neighbourhood healthier, safer, greener or more inclusive?

Final output: concise concept board/canvas + maximum 4-minute pitch.

Who can join — and why it matters

Who can join?

Secondary school students aged 14–19, especially girls who are curious about STEM, innovation, sustainability, cities, mobility or future careers.

Students will work in teams, supported by facilitators, mentors, teachers and inspiring professionals.

No need to be “a science person” already.

14–19 YEARSFREE TO JOIN

What students gain

  • new ideas about STEM and future careers
  • more confidence in their own voice
  • teamwork and problem-solving experience
  • certificate of participation
  • memories from a fun, colourful and inspiring day

Why schools should care

The event connects classroom learning to real-world challenges and shows STEM as creative, social and useful. It supports confidence, digital skills, sustainability thinking and entrepreneurial problem-solving.
